# Third-party dependencies

KestrelOS integrates with external NVIDIA driver objects, headers, and device
firmware in the local full build. Those packages are not redistributed here;
obtain them from their respective publishers and follow their licenses.

The local development tree also has generated glyph headers made from
commercial fonts and a device VBIOS test fixture. They are excluded from this
repository. The included source and font-generation scripts are provided for
review; no license for the original KestrelOS code is granted by publication.
